# Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge

A one-line note (132 characters) a smart fridge leaves for its owner, for the make.fun bounty
[Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-notes-from-a-passive-aggressive-fridge-83c00ad7).

> Your leftovers have been here nine days; I've started charging them rent, since at least they don't open me at 2 a.m. just to stare.

`main.ts` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt`. The joke is a small domestic grudge — leftovers left for nine days — answered the way a smug appliance would: by charging rent, and by pointing out that the leftovers, unlike the owner, never open the fridge at 2 a.m. just to stare.
